About I Love Caulk

{{> bead-line}}

We started I Love Caulk because caulk is the most underrated product in the house. It keeps water out of your walls, drafts out of your bedroom, and bugs out of your pantry, and almost nobody explains how to choose it or use it. So we do. Seriously.

{{joint}}

What we believe

  • There's a caulk for every crack. Most caulk failures aren't bad caulk. They're the right caulk in the wrong joint.
  • Prep is most of the job. Clean, dry, and free of old caulk beats any miracle formula.
  • The tube is the boss. Formulas change. Temperatures, cure times, and paint windows on the label win every argument, including arguments with us.
  • Caulk is backup, not a building system. It supports flashing, drainage, and good design. It can't replace them.
  • Learning should be fun. Hence the game, the crossword, and the personality quiz. We are serious about caulk. We are not solemn about it.

How we pick products

Our reviews and Seal of Approval picks are editorial summaries: manufacturer specifications, the product's intended use, and the experience widely reported by pros and DIYers. They are not lab tests, and we say so.

No brand pays for placement, and we don't use affiliate links. If that ever changes, we'll label it clearly on every page it touches.

Products and formulas change. When the label and this site disagree, trust the label, and tell us so we can fix our page.

How the site pays for itself

I Love Caulk is supported by advertising from Google AdSense. Ads are labeled "Advertisement" and kept away from game controls and buttons. Advertisers have no say in what we write or recommend.
