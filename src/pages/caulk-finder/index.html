How Rosie decides

The Finder works through the same questions a good hardware-store veteran would ask, in this order of priority:

  1. Is water getting in right now?

    Nothing sticks to a running leak. Masonry leaks get hydraulic cement first; everything else gets dried out before sealing.

  2. What's the joint made of, and where is it?

    Concrete, masonry, metal gutters, roofs, and soundproofing each have a specialist. Everything else is decided by the next three questions.

  3. How wet does it get?

    Daily water means silicone. Occasional splashes can take siliconized acrylic. Dry joints can take anything.

  4. Will it be painted?

    Standard silicone never takes paint, so a painted joint needs acrylic, elastomeric, polyurethane, or a hybrid.

  5. How big is it, and does it move?

    Wide joints need backer rod. Moving joints need a sealant with a real movement rating. Big movement rules out plain acrylic.
