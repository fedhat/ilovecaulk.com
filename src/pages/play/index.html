Why a caulk site has games

A good bead comes down to three things: steady speed, steady pressure, and letting off the trigger early. Reading that is easy. Feeling it is a skill, and Bead Master lets you practice the feel without ruining a bathtub.

The puzzles do the same for vocabulary. Once you know what three-sided adhesion, open time and thrust ratio mean, the caulk aisle stops being mysterious. When you're ready for the real thing, read how to lay a perfect bead.
