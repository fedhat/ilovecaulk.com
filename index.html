Questions we get every day

What's the difference between caulk and sealant?

In the store, not much: the words overlap. In the trade, "caulk" usually means the stiffer, paintable stuff for small, still joints, and "sealant" means flexible, high-performance products for joints that move. The full breakdown.

Can I put new caulk over old caulk?

Please don't. New caulk sticks to the old bead, and the old bead is already failing. Cut it out, clean the joint, and start fresh. Here's how.

How long before I can shower after caulking?

Follow the tube. Many kitchen and bath silicones are water-ready in 30 minutes to a few hours, but a full 24 hours is the safe bet for a bead that lasts.

Why won't paint stick to my caulk?

It's almost certainly silicone. Standard silicone repels paint. Cut it out and use a paintable acrylic, polyurethane, or hybrid.

What's the best caulk gun?

A smooth-rod, dripless gun with a decent thrust ratio beats a cheap ratchet gun every time. Our gun guide explains why.
