      How to play

      Click or tap a square and start typing. The yellow square is where your next letter goes; the seafoam squares are the rest of the word. Your progress saves on this device, so you can walk away mid-bead and come back later.

      Prefer pen and paper? The printout skips the buttons and your answers.

      Letters
      Fill the square and move to the next one
      Arrow keys
      Move around the grid (the first press turns you to face that way)
      Space, or tap the square again
      Switch between Across and Down
      Tab / Shift+Tab
      Jump to the next or previous clue
      Backspace
      Erase and step back
      Escape
      Leave the grid and go to the buttons
